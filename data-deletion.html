Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 16 October 2026

If you have signed in to Khan Marketplace with Facebook, or connected a Facebook Page to your shop, you can request deletion of the data we received from Facebook at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ramimamdouhali@gmail.com with the subject line "Facebook Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email address associated with your Khan Marketplace account (or the Facebook email if you signed in with Facebook).
  3. We will confirm receipt within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 days.

What will be deleted

  • Your Facebook-linked account information (name, email, profile photo if you signed in with Facebook).
  • Any Facebook Page access tokens stored on our servers.
  • Any records linking your Facebook account or Page to your Khan Marketplace account.

What may be retained

We may retain transactional records (orders, invoices, payouts) as required by financial compliance regulations, for the period required by law.

Alternative: revoke access directly

You may also revoke our access to your Facebook account or Page at any time via Facebook:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find Khan Marketplace for Business (or Khan for login) in the list.
  3. Click Remove.

Revoking access immediately stops us from publishing to your Page or replying to comments. Facebook will also notify us of the revocation and we will delete the stored token.
