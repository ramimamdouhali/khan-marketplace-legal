Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Khan Marketplace ("we", "us") operates an online classifieds platform. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, profile photo.
  • Facebook login data (optional): if you sign in with Facebook, we receive your public profile name, email, and profile picture.
  • Facebook Page data (merchants only, optional): if you connect a Facebook Page to your shop, we receive a Page access token that allows us to publish product listings to that Page and to read and reply to comments on those posts. You control this connection and may disconnect it at any time from your shop settings.
  • Listing data: the products, photos, descriptions, and prices you publish.
  • Transaction data: orders, payments, and delivery details associated with sales through the platform.

How we use your data

  • To provide the marketplace service.
  • To publish your listings and manage your shop.
  • If you enable Facebook Page integration, to publish listings to your Page and to automatically reply to comments on those posts with the price of the referenced item.
  • To process payments and payouts.
  • To send notifications you have opted into.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Facebook (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — only when you explicitly connect a Facebook Page. In that case, we transmit your listing content to Facebook in order to publish it on your Page, and we receive comment events from Facebook to reply on your behalf.
  • Payment providers — to process payments you initiate.
  • Shipping companies you select during checkout.

Data retention

We retain account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. See Data Deletion Instructions.
